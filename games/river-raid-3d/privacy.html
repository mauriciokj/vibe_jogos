Privacy

Last updated: August 5, 2026. The game does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

Data kept on your device

Your browser may store your pilot name, local high score, audio and graphics preferences, control layout, custom positions, and configuration progress. This data remains in your browser's local storage and can be removed by clearing the site's data.

Online leaderboard

When you choose to submit a score, we send the name you entered, score, round, bridges destroyed, evasions, distance, game version, and daily challenge identifier when applicable. This information is used only to operate and protect the global and daily leaderboards.

Technical data and metrics

The Vercel-hosted version may use aggregated access metrics from Vercel Web Analytics. Partner portals may also receive gameplay events under their own policies. Rio de Aço does not enable advertising until an advertising partner is configured.

Services used

  • Vercel: hosting, leaderboard API, and aggregated metrics.
  • Vercel KV: storage for top leaderboard results.
  • Distribution portal: only when the game is opened inside a partner platform.

Control and removal

You can play without submitting a score, use a nickname, and clear preferences through your browser. For questions or requests related to an online result, use the support page and provide the name shown on the leaderboard.

Children and content

The game does not request age, address, phone number, precise location, or payment information. Guardians should review the rules and policies of the portal where the game is published.
