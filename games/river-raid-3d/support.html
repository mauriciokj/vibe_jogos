Support

Current documentation version: 1.20.0.

Computer controls

  • A/D or arrows: move sideways.
  • W/S or arrows: accelerate and brake.
  • Space: fire; C: change camera.
  • Q: barrel roll; E: flare when available.
  • P: pause; M: sound; F: fullscreen where available.

Mobile controls

Use LAYOUT 1/2 to change the arrangement. In SETTINGS you can adjust size, visibility, graphics, audio, and group positions. Your choices are saved on the device.

If the game freezes or runs slowly

  1. Open SETTINGS → GRAPHICS → AUTO or LOW.
  2. Close other heavy tabs and applications.
  3. Update your browser and reload the page.
  4. If audio stops after switching apps, tap the game again.

Leaderboard unavailable

The game keeps a local leaderboard when the connection or API is unavailable. Try again later to submit the result online.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the project repository. Include your device, browser, version shown in the corner, camera, round, and a screenshot when possible.

Privacy

See what stays on your device and what is sent to the leaderboard in the Privacy Policy.
